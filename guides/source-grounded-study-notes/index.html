Build a source-grounded study companion

Organize notes, PDFs, and source material into study questions and explainers.

LearningBeginnerEstimated first setup: 20–40 minTool: NotebookLM

What you can accomplish

Review complex documents with less manual organization.

Your first build: step by step

  1. Collect the readings or documents you are authorized to use.
  2. Add them to a NotebookLM notebook.
  3. Ask for an outline, quiz questions, and a comparison of ideas.
  4. Verify important answers against the original material.

What to watch out for

Practical caution: Source-grounding reduces some errors but does not guarantee correctness.

How to evaluate the result

Run a small test on representative inputs. Check accuracy, the time it actually saves, whether a person can correct mistakes, and whether the workflow achieves the intended outcome. Increase automation only after you can explain how failures are detected and handled.

Editor's note: The workflow is an Agent Lab implementation suggestion, not a vendor-endorsed tutorial. Setup times are editorial estimates; actual work varies by tool, account access, and complexity.
