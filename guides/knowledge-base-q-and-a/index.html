Make an internal knowledge assistant

Let your team ask questions about approved SOPs with answers that point to source documents.

OperationsIntermediateEstimated first setup: 2–4 hoursTool: OpenAI API

What you can accomplish

Reduce repeat questions and speed up access to internal instructions.

Your first build: step by step

  1. Collect and approve current SOPs and policies.
  2. Choose a file-search or retrieval workflow with access controls.
  3. Require answers to reference the exact supporting document.
  4. Test missing-information scenarios and add escalation to a human owner.

What to watch out for

Practical caution: Access controls and data governance matter more than a fluent response.

How to evaluate the result

Run a small test on representative inputs. Check accuracy, the time it actually saves, whether a person can correct mistakes, and whether the workflow achieves the intended outcome. Increase automation only after you can explain how failures are detected and handled.

Editor's note: The workflow is an Agent Lab implementation suggestion, not a vendor-endorsed tutorial. Setup times are editorial estimates; actual work varies by tool, account access, and complexity.
