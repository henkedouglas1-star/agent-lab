Produce a competitor landscape brief

Compare publicly documented positioning, pricing pages, and product differences.

ResearchBeginnerEstimated first setup: 1–2 hoursTool: ChatGPT + Web Search

What you can accomplish

Find opportunities for differentiation without relying on hearsay.

Your first build: step by step

  1. List the customer job and competitors to compare.
  2. Collect official product pages and current pricing disclosures.
  3. Summarize differences with links and dates accessed.
  4. Flag unverified claims and validate before making decisions.

What to watch out for

Practical caution: Competitor pricing and features change. Date every comparison.

How to evaluate the result

Run a small test on representative inputs. Check accuracy, the time it actually saves, whether a person can correct mistakes, and whether the workflow achieves the intended outcome. Increase automation only after you can explain how failures are detected and handled.
